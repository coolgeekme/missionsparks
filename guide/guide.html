MissionSparks

Add the Moment button to your home screen

The Moment screen (Quick Log) lets you mark a moment Positive or Negative in one tap. Add MissionSparks to your phone's home screen and it opens like an app — straight to that screen, no typing a web address.

This is the Moment screen. Tap Positive or Negative — one tap and it's logged.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Go to missionsparks.com and sign in.
missionsparks.com — tap Sign in.
  1. Tap the Share button — the square with an up arrow, at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Scroll the menu that slides up and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top-right corner.
  4. Done. The MissionSparks icon is on your home screen — tap it and it opens straight to the Moment screen.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open Chrome on your phone.
  2. Go to missionsparks.com and sign in.
  3. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  4. Tap Add to Home screen — on newer phones it may say Install app.
  5. Tap Add (or Install).
  6. Done. Tap the icon to open straight to the Moment screen.

Good to know

One tap, nothing else. The icon opens directly to the Moment screen.
It remembers you. Once you're signed in, you won't need to sign in again.
Rename it (iPhone). Before tapping "Add," tap the name field to change what it's called.
